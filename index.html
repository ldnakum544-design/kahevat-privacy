Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kahevat - Gujarati Proverbs ("the App"), developed by Devisha Foundation, collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects minimal information necessary to function:

  • Leaderboard Name (optional): In the Quiz section, you have the option to enter a name for the Weekly Leaderboard. If you choose to provide this, the name is stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore (a Firebase service) and is used solely to display your ranking. You can skip this; the quiz can be played without providing a name.
  • Feedback data (optional): If you voluntarily submit feedback through the Settings screen, we collect your name (optional), feedback type, and message. This is stored securely and accessible only to the developer.
  • Device preferences: App settings such as dark mode, font size, and notification preferences are stored locally on your device using shared preferences. This data never leaves your device.
  • Favourite & Suggest proverbs: Proverbs you mark as favourite and suggest to us are collected for app improvement only.

2. Quiz Leaderboard & Data Deletion

If you submit a name for the Quiz Leaderboard, it is saved to Google Cloud Firestore to display your weekly ranking to other users.

How to delete your data: If you wish to have your name removed from the leaderboard database, please contact us at kahevat@gmail.com with the subject "Leaderboard Data Deletion". We will permanently delete your entry from our database within 7 days.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your email address or phone number.
  • We do not track your precise location.
  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information automatically.
  • We do not sell or share your data with any third-party advertisers (beyond standard AdMob SDK behavior).

4. Google AdMob (Advertisements)

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized ads by adjusting your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Internet Access

The App uses internet access to load the latest proverbs, submit leaderboard scores, and display ads. No personal data is transmitted during the general proverb loading process — only the proverb data is downloaded to your device.

6. Notifications

If you enable notifications, the App schedules local notifications on your device (at 8:00 AM and 9:00 PM) to display a daily proverb. These notifications are generated locally and do not involve any external server or data transmission. You can disable notifications at any time from the App's Settings screen or from your device's notification settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App contains no content harmful to children.

8. Data Security

All data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's built-in security. Data submitted to Firestore (like leaderboard names) is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored securely using Google's infrastructure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
